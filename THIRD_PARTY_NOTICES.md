# Third-party components

- **DINOv2** — [Meta Platforms, Inc. and affiliates](https://github.com/facebookresearch/dinov2). The bundled inference code is covered by [Apache-2.0](third_party/dinov2/LICENSE). The `hubconf.py` entry point is adapted for ViT-S/14.
- **CBAM** — channel and spatial attention implemented following the [authors' reference](https://github.com/Jongchan/attention-module).
- **CECF** — uses cosine attention scaling and depthwise convolution concepts from Swin Transformer V2 and CvT.
- **Dependencies** — PyTorch, NumPy, OpenCV, SciPy, scikit-learn, and pytest retain their respective licenses.
- **Datasets** — AeBAD-S and KSDD2 are obtained separately under the dataset authors' terms.
